01 / about

About me

Most people sitting on data don't have a data team. They have a spreadsheet, a question, and no time to learn SQL. That gap is what I like working on.

I've spent 2+ years turning sales, supply chain, and customer data into decisions. At AgriGain Alliance I was the person in the middle, digging into supply chain and ERP data for pricing, distribution, and sales trends, building Python and Airflow pipelines to clean it, then Power BI dashboards that helped cut marketing costs by 17% and lift sales by 20%. The dashboard was the easy part. Getting to the right question was the hard part.

So at TwinAI I built the thing I wished we'd had: GenBI, where you upload a file and get charts, KPIs, and written insights back with no SQL. Now I build RAG systems, multi-agent workflows, and LLM analytics products, and I care just as much about the unglamorous parts: clean data, validation, evaluation, and getting it deployed.
